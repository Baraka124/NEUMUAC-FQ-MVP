## Why another source review was necessary

The archived application is a dense scientific workstation, not merely variant search and a dashboard. The Vue replacement already improves typed boundaries, scoped link navigation, result truthfulness, cancellation, citations and privacy. Yet "new workspace exists" is **not** functional parity. Review the actual functions, their user task, evidence dependencies, side effects and failure modes. Our decision rule is: **recover the benefit, reject unsafe assumptions, then design a better research interaction**.

## Concrete interface improvement rules

1. **Research question first:** Search → unambiguous record → evidence/provenance → uncertainty → the next permitted action. A researcher need not visit an unrelated dashboard before reaching the work.
2. **Meaningful distinctions:** recorded/not recorded, loading/error/denied, known/uncertain, selected suggestion/confirmed identity, conflict detected/recorded resolution, project stage/scientific decision are different states.
3. **Disclosure with purpose:** 10 references per page/preview, contextual `i` for long explanations, no ornamental repeated headings, no nesting endless scroll panels. Full source access retained via pagination.
4. **Clinical humility:** labels, statuses and metrics must identify their authority and denominator. Any source-derived quality evaluation needs an independently reviewed rubric. A clinical-looking badge does not make something validated.
5. **More than nostalgia:** reuse original **scientific questions and workflows**, not its modal stack, search auto-normalization side effects, hidden local caches, optimistic locks or heuristic patient predictions.

## Proposed decision sequence (not delivered by this audit)

### 4F-13K — Source-supported discovery recovery (first)
- Evaluate documented historical alias, eponymic, positional, fuzzy and multi-term differential examples against actual modern search behavior. Publish a reviewed exception list.
- Keep low-confidence leads **only** in suggestions, never scientific canvas, saved research facts or geographic hit streams.
- Improve explicit miss/ambiguous-input feedback without persisted search analytics or synthetic evidence.
- Tests: exact `1001+11C/T` and other real recorded names, blocked allele-phase strings, partial-prefix non-selection, ambiguous alias negatives, cancellation, keyboard/mobile, no geography event side effects.
- No new authority claims, no production mutations.

### 4F-14A — Evidence and source verification depth
- Review source identity/licensing, DOI/PMID consistency and optional study-design metadata as **recorded provenance**, with no quality-score-based automatic ranking.
- Confirm stale/error/denied/empty and recent fetch timestamps; preserve full real-data references and functional assay detail only when catalog-backed and source-specific.

### 4F-14B — Research workflow composition
- Design task-driven dossier/coverage/comparison journeys based on real records; consolidate duplicated headers and unproductive cross-links. Validate at phone and desktop widths with keyboard support.

### Later gated workstreams
- **Helios & human curation:** secured backend + immutable review transactions before controls.
- **Privacy-safe gap/region observatory:** research priorities and opt-in aggregation with denominators and removal semantics, distinct from patient locations.
- **Research exports:** reproducible provenance-first briefs/print templates; patient/IRB paths only under separate approvals.
- **Operations:** server-authorized import, dry-run, reconciliation, idempotent retry and data-retention controls.
